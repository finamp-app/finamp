Finamp is a <br>free</br> Jellyfin music player for Android, iOS and Desktop. It allows you to stream and download the music you own. It has a modern but <br>thought-through</br> design, a ton of features, is customizable, and respects your <br>privacy</br>!

You <b>need</b> access to a Jellyfin server (https://jellyfin.org) or you <b>won't be able to use Finamp at all</b>!

Finamp comes with a bunch of features, including these:

- Gapless playback
- Shallow library browsing, to quickly get into your music
- Volume normalization (aka "ReplayGain") for tracks and albums
- Lyrics, time-synced lyrics, and even word-by-word cue highlighting
- Flexible transcoding options
  - Choose between AAC, OPUS, Vorbis, and FLAC codecs
  - Supports downmixing & lossless transcoding to FLAC, for maximum compatibility
- Dedicated offline mode with automatic connectivity detection
- A powerful download system that does it all
  - Download individual tracks, albums, entire artists & genres, or your whole library
  - Download all your favorites in one go
  - Download the newest additions to your library
  - Transcode all downloads to take up less space on your device
  - Customizable download locations
  - And keeps all of these downloads up-to-date automatically as things change on the server
- Advanced playback options:
  - Choose between shuffling or playing in order
  - Choose if you want to play now, add to "Next Up", or after everything else
- Android Auto & Apple CarPlay support
- Desktop & Tablet Support with a split-screen layout
- A Customizable Home Tab
- List views & grid views with adjustable density
- Adaptive app color theme based on your system colors
  - (i.e. "Material You" & equivalents for other platforms)
- Easily switch to a previous queue from hours or days ago
- A radio feature that always keeps the music playing with something related
  - And you get to choose between 5 different modes to cater to *your* listening habits
- AudioMuse integration (https://github.com/NeptuneHub/AudioMuse-AI) for an enhanced radio mode powered by sonic analysis
  - You'll have to set up AudioMuse and install the AudioMuse Jellyfin plugin to use this in Finamp
- Solid customization options, including tabs that can be hidden and reordered
- Support for several languages
- Jellyfin 12+ compatibility
  - But should also work on older versions down to 10.8.x!
- and a lot more!
