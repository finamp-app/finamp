Finamp's redesign is now finally stable and has been released to F-Droid!

Here's what's new:

<ul>
<li>Customizable Home Tab</li>
<li>Many redesigned screens & menus</li>
<li>Lyrics support</li>
<li>Volume normalization</li>
<li>Adaptive theme</li>
<li>Auto-restore queue</li>
<li>New playback options</li>
<li>Vastly improved download system</li>
<li>Automatic offline mode</li>
<li>Android Auto & CarPlay (<strong>only if installed via Play Store or enabled in Android Auto Developer Settings!</strong>)</li>
<li>Support for custom certs & mTLS</li>
<li>Desktop & Tablet Support</li>
<li>Tons of bug fixes</li>
<li>Jellyfin 12.0 compatibility</li>
<li>and much, much more!</li>
</ul>
